Weekly mix, three directions

The same app in three different styles: build a weekly album mix of 4 to 8 hours, look back at past weeks, and keep albums as your own records with Spotify as one linked service. All data is sample data.

Rotation

Rotation

A radio programmer's desk. It is dark, dense and keyboard-first, and a program clock shows runtime and genre balance in one ring.
House Label

House Label

A one-person record label. Each week is a release whose cover sets itself from the albums on it, with row height equal to runtime.
Crate & Counter

Crate & Counter

A record-shop dig. You flip through four crates, pull sleeves onto a shelf of spines, and keep each week as a receipt.
